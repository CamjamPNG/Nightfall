Build a quiet soundscape from independent sound layers. Nightfall is a free,
offline sleep audio mixer with no accounts, advertising, purchases or tracking.

Mix as many layers as your device can reliably handle. Each layer has its own
gain, enable, mute, solo, pan, filters, EQ and fade controls. There is no fixed
nine-layer limit. Performance warnings help you recognize device overload.

Choose colored noise, synthesized weather, water, wind, fire and indoor
textures, or bundled field recordings of birds, crickets, frogs and more.
Synthesized environments are labeled honestly. Recording loops run for at
least three minutes before repeating, with smooth transitions.

Create original ambient and lo-fi music on your device. Choose a musical key,
major or minor scale, A4 tuning, tempo, note density, instrument texture,
reverb and delay. Saved seeds reproduce compositions. A4=432 Hz and 440 Hz
are musical tuning references, not treatments or established sleep therapies.

Features include:
• Independent layers with a master volume and clipping protection
• Fifteen built-in mixes and locally saved user presets
• Preset favorites, duplication, renaming and JSON import/export
• Sleep timers with gentle final fades and independent layer stop schedules
• Background media playback and notification/lock-screen controls
• Android-selected Bluetooth, wired headphone or speaker output
• Optional light theme and reduced-motion waveform
• No INTERNET permission; all playback and generation run locally

Android battery policies and hardware affect long sessions. Keep listening
levels comfortable. Optional binaural tones require stereo headphones; sleep
stage and healing benefits are not established.
